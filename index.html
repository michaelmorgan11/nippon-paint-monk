The challenges the AR team is facing.

Short pay, no reason given

The remittance tells you which invoice. It does not tell you which part, or why. The variance sits on the balance sheet until someone works out whether it is pricing, an RGA or a credit memo.

Monk matches the payment, sizes the shortfall against the invoice, and fires a playbook to the customer's AP within hours asking for the line item and reason, then routes it to sales with the detail attached.
Bounced or stale AP contact

Sales only deals with procurement. The AP contact changes and nobody hears about it, so outreach looks covered but never happened.

Monk detects the bounce or departure reply, queries Apollo and ZoomInfo, writes the new contact into the customer record, and resumes the thread without a gap.
Portal rejection on Toyota or Honda

Roughly 40 of 500 invoices and a similar share of revenue. Nothing notifies the team, so the clock starts late on the two largest accounts.

Monk scans both connections on a schedule, double-attaches the PO and supporting docs to cut rejection odds, and works the error the day it appears.
Bill of lading required before payment

One customer needs proof of delivery alongside the invoice. Operations forwards it to billing, and billing forwards it on.

Monk attaches anything emailed to the connected inbox to the right invoice and sends it with the next touch, with nobody on your team in the middle.
